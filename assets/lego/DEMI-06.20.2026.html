Demi X

Demi is a sweet girl with some trust issues. Despite that, she is a very easy going gal that fits right into just about any pack. She requires a LOT of time and patience if you want to get hands on her and gain trust. When you do, she loves sitting in your lap and getting all the pets for hours. She would prefer a quiet home without much foot traffic and the less she has to leave her home, the better. You could say she's a bit of a home body and rather stay that way. Outtings can be extra stressful for this poor girl that has reservations about trusting people. She is happiest with another dog companion she can glue herself to and an understanding owner that lets her have her space. She won't be a cuddle buddy or one you can take to the park, but she will be a steady, low maintenance dog that you can always have when at home. She does well going into her crate at night and is easy to let out into a yard and back in when she's done with her business. Consider giving this sweet girl a forever home she can be comfortable in.
