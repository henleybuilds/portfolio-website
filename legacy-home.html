What’s Happening Knoxville

What’s Happening Knoxville is a local news and events channel built to actually be useful to this community — covering events, hidden gems, and the people and places that make Knoxville worth living in.

The most common feedback I get is “why aren’t you using real photos of Knoxville?” You’re right and I’m already working through that myself. Real photos from people who actually live here make it better though — if you have any you’d like to contribute send them to whknoxville@gmail.com and they may end up in a future video.

I’m also building a way for local small businesses to get free coverage on the channel. More on that soon.

Have a suggestion for an event, place, or story? whknoxville@gmail.com
